Check Your Home Loan Eligibility

Your eligibility depends on your financial profile, credit history, income and the property you're planning to purchase.

  • Credit Score: Lenders review your credit history and score to assess your creditworthiness.
  • Income & Employment: Stable, verifiable income helps demonstrate your ability to repay the loan.
  • Debt-to-Income Ratio: Your existing monthly debt payments are considered alongside your income.
  • Down Payment: The amount you can contribute upfront may influence the loan options available to you.
  • Property Details: The property's value, condition and intended use can affect lender approval.
  • Loan Amount & Terms: Your requested borrowing amount and repayment period are assessed against your financial circumstances.
